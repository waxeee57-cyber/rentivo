## Description — ENGLISH (4000 chars max)

**Rent Anything, Anywhere in Europe.**

Rentivo is Europe's all-in-one rental marketplace — connecting travelers with verified hosts and professional operators offering cars, villas, boats, motorcycles, and more. Whether you're planning a sun-soaked road trip along the Adriatic coast, a lakeside villa escape, or a day out on the open water, Rentivo puts thousands of listings at your fingertips with instant booking and secure payments.

**For Travelers**

Finding and booking the perfect rental has never been easier. Browse hundreds of verified vehicles, watercraft, and holiday properties across six European countries. Every listing includes real photos, honest reviews, and transparent pricing — no hidden fees, no surprises.

- Instant booking: confirm your rental in under two minutes
- Verified hosts and operators: every listing reviewed for quality and accuracy
- Secure payments powered by Stripe — your card details are never stored by Rentivo
- In-app messaging: communicate directly with your host before and after booking
- Digital damage reports: transparent photo documentation protects both sides
- Flexible cancellation options shown upfront on every listing
- Manage all your bookings from a single, clean dashboard

Rentivo covers Croatia, Hungary, Italy, Spain, Portugal, and Greece — with new destinations added every month. Whether you need a sleek city car in Budapest, a catamaran in Split, or a hilltop villa in Tuscany, the inventory is ready and waiting.

**For Hosts**

Turn your idle asset into a reliable income stream. Listing your vehicle, boat, or property on Rentivo takes less than ten minutes. Set your own price, availability, and house rules — Rentivo handles the rest.

- List cars, motorcycles, boats, jet skis, villas, and apartments
- Stripe-powered automatic payouts — earnings land in your account within two business days of a completed rental
- Instant booking or request-to-book — your choice
- In-app messaging keeps every conversation documented and dispute-free
- Damage reports with photo evidence, so your asset is always protected
- Real-time booking notifications and calendar management
- Performance dashboard: track earnings, occupancy rate, and guest reviews at a glance

Joining as a host is free. Rentivo takes a small service fee only when a booking is confirmed — so there's zero risk to getting started.

**For Professional Operators**

Running a rental fleet? Rentivo's operator tier is built for scale. Manage multiple listings, vehicles, and booking calendars from a single operator dashboard designed for efficiency.

- Multi-vehicle fleet management from one account
- Advanced analytics: revenue trends, utilisation rates, peak season insights
- Bulk availability calendar editing
- Priority listing placement and operator badge for trust signals
- Custom pricing rules: seasonal rates, minimum rental periods, early-bird discounts
- Dedicated support channel for operator-tier accounts

Whether you run three cars or thirty boats, Rentivo gives you the professional tools to maximise revenue with minimum overhead.

**Why Rentivo?**

- 500+ verified listings across Europe
- 98% guest satisfaction rate
- 4.8-star average rating from real, verified guests
- Available in English and Hungarian, with more languages coming
- GDPR-compliant: you own your data, always
- No dark patterns — pricing is transparent from search to checkout

**Get started in minutes.**

Download Rentivo, create a free account, and book your first rental today. Europe is waiting — with the right vehicle, the journey becomes the destination.
